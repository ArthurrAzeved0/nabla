Determine a solução geral do sistema:

$$
\begin{cases} \dfrac{dx}{dt} = 4x + 5y \\[4pt] \dfrac{dy}{dt} = -2x + 6y \end{cases}
$$

<Gabarito>
$\lambda = 5 \pm 3i$

$$
X(t) = c_1e^{5t}\begin{pmatrix}\cos 3t + 3\operatorname{sen} 3t\\ 2\cos 3t\end{pmatrix} + c_2e^{5t}\begin{pmatrix}-3\cos 3t + \operatorname{sen} 3t\\ 2\operatorname{sen} 3t\end{pmatrix}
$$
</Gabarito>

<Passos>
**Passo 1 —** $A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 5 \\ -2 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$. Autovalores:

$$
\det(A - \lambda I) = \lambda^2 - 10\lambda + 34 = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; \lambda = \frac{10 \pm \sqrt{100 - 136}}{2} = 5 \pm 3i
$$

**Passo 2 —** Autovetor para $\lambda_1 = 5 + 3i$:

$$
\begin{pmatrix} -1-3i & 5 \\ -2 & 1-3i \end{pmatrix}K = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; 2k_1 = (1-3i)k_2
$$

Com $k_2 = 2$: $k_1 = 1 - 3i$, então

$$
K_1 = \begin{pmatrix}1-3i\\2\end{pmatrix} = \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix}}_{B_1} + i\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}-3\\0\end{pmatrix}}_{B_2}
$$

**Passo 3 —** Com $\alpha = 5$ e $\beta = 3$, as soluções reais são:

$$
X_1 = e^{5t}\big(B_1\cos 3t - B_2\operatorname{sen} 3t\big) = e^{5t}\begin{pmatrix}\cos 3t + 3\operatorname{sen} 3t\\ 2\cos 3t\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
X_2 = e^{5t}\big(B_2\cos 3t + B_1\operatorname{sen} 3t\big) = e^{5t}\begin{pmatrix}-3\cos 3t + \operatorname{sen} 3t\\ 2\operatorname{sen} 3t\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Passo 4 —** Solução geral (espiral instável, pois $\operatorname{Re}\lambda = 5 \gt  0$):

$$
\boxed{X(t) = c_1e^{5t}\begin{pmatrix}\cos 3t + 3\operatorname{sen} 3t\\ 2\cos 3t\end{pmatrix} + c_2e^{5t}\begin{pmatrix}-3\cos 3t + \operatorname{sen} 3t\\ 2\operatorname{sen} 3t\end{pmatrix}}
$$
</Passos>
