Considere o sistema:

$$
\begin{cases} x' = -2x - 3y \\ y' = 3x - 2y \end{cases}
$$

Escreva o sistema na forma vetorial $X' = AX$ e obtenha a sua solução geral.

<Gabarito>
$\lambda = -2 \pm 3i$;   $X(t) = c_1e^{-2t}\begin{pmatrix}\cos 3t\\ \operatorname{sen} 3t\end{pmatrix} + c_2e^{-2t}\begin{pmatrix}\operatorname{sen} 3t\\ -\cos 3t\end{pmatrix}$.
</Gabarito>

<Passos>
**Passo 1 —** $X' = \begin{pmatrix} -2 & -3 \\ 3 & -2 \end{pmatrix}X$. Autovalores:

$$
\det(A-\lambda I) = (\lambda+2)^2 + 9 = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; \lambda = -2 \pm 3i
$$

**Passo 2 —** Autovetor para $\lambda_1 = -2 + 3i$:

$$
\begin{pmatrix} -3i & -3 \\ 3 & -3i \end{pmatrix}K = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; k_1 = ik_2 \;\Rightarrow\; K_1 = \begin{pmatrix}1\\-i\end{pmatrix} = \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}}_{B_1} + i\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}0\\-1\end{pmatrix}}_{B_2}
$$

**Passo 3 —** Com $\alpha = -2$ e $\beta = 3$, as duas soluções reais são:

$$
X_1 = e^{-2t}\big(B_1\cos 3t - B_2\operatorname{sen} 3t\big) = e^{-2t}\begin{pmatrix}\cos 3t\\ \operatorname{sen} 3t\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
X_2 = e^{-2t}\big(B_2\cos 3t + B_1\operatorname{sen} 3t\big) = e^{-2t}\begin{pmatrix}\operatorname{sen} 3t\\ -\cos 3t\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Passo 4 —** Solução geral (uma espiral estável, pois $\operatorname{Re}\lambda = -2 \lt  0$):

$$
\boxed{X(t) = c_1e^{-2t}\begin{pmatrix}\cos 3t\\ \operatorname{sen} 3t\end{pmatrix} + c_2e^{-2t}\begin{pmatrix}\operatorname{sen} 3t\\ -\cos 3t\end{pmatrix}}
$$
</Passos>
